### Recommendation

Given the client's budget of $1,200 for accommodation over 10 nights, the most cost-effective and highly-rated options would be:

1. **The Prince Park Tower Tokyo** - Offers a balance of luxury and affordability within the budget.
2. **Hotel Chinzanso Tokyo** - Provides a luxurious stay with a serene environment, though slightly higher in price.
3. **The Ritz-Carlton, Kyoto** - For the Kyoto portion of the trip, this offers unparalleled luxury and location but is at the higher end of the budget.

**Final Recommendation:**
- **Primary Choice:** The Prince Park Tower Tokyo (Tokyo) and The Ritz-Carlton, Kyoto (Kyoto)
- **Secondary Choice:** Hotel Chinzanso Tokyo (Tokyo) if budget allows or preferences shift towards a quieter, more luxurious experience.

These options provide excellent value, amenities, and locations that align with the client's requirements and preferences.
