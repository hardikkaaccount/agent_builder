### Accommodation Options Research

Based on the client's requirements, I have researched and compared various accommodation options in Tokyo and Kyoto. Below are the top three options that best meet the client's needs, including budget, preferences, and special requirements.

---

#### **Top 3 Accommodation Options**

---

### 1. **Hotel Gracery Shinjuku (Tokyo)**

**Location:**  
1-19-1 Kabukicho, Shinjuku City, Tokyo 160-0021, Japan

**Price Range:**  
Approx. $150 - $200 per night

**Amenities:**  
- Free Wi-Fi
- Breakfast included
- Fitness center
- On-site restaurant
- 24-hour front desk
- Close to public transportation

**Pros:**  
- Central location in Shinjuku, close to shopping districts and public transport.
- Modern amenities and comfortable rooms.
- Excellent customer service.

**Cons:**  
- Can be quite busy due to its central location.

**Recommendation:**  
Ideal for those who want to be in the heart of Tokyo’s bustling entertainment and shopping district.

---

### 2. **HOTEL THE FLAG Shinsaibashi (Osaka)**

**Location:**  
3-2-23 Higashishinsaibashi, Chuo Ward, Osaka 542-0083, Japan

**Price Range:**  
Approx. $130 - $180 per night

**Amenities:**  
- Free Wi-Fi
- Breakfast included
- Spa and wellness center
- Bar/Lounge area
- 24-hour front desk
- Close to Shinsaibashi shopping area

**Pros:**  
- Located in a prime shopping district.
- Modern and clean facilities.
- Excellent access to public transportation.

**Cons:**  
- Some rooms may be small.

**Recommendation:**  
Great for shoppers and those who enjoy modern amenities with easy access to local attractions.

---

### 3. **Ryokan Hiramatsu (Kyoto)**

**Location:**  
608-0871 Kyoto, Higashiyama Ward, Sanjo-bashi Higashizume, Japan

**Price Range:**  
Approx. $200 - $250 per night

**Amenities:**  
- Free Wi-Fi
- Traditional multi-course dinner and breakfast included
- Hot spring baths (Onsen)
- Garden
- Concierge service

**Pros:**  
- Authentic Ryokan experience with traditional Japanese architecture and hospitality.
- Located near major cultural sites like Kiyomizu-dera Temple.
- Tranquil and relaxing atmosphere.

**Cons:**  
- Higher price point compared to standard hotels.
- May require more walking to reach certain amenities.

**Recommendation:**  
Perfect for those seeking an immersive cultural experience with traditional Japanese hospitality.

---

### Conclusion and Recommendation

Given the client's budget of $1200 for accommodation over 10 nights, the average nightly rate should be around $120. 

**Recommended Option:**  
**Hotel Gracery Shinjuku** offers a balance of modern amenities, central location, and is within the budget. 

If the client prefers a more cultural and traditional experience and is willing to allocate a slightly higher budget for Kyoto nights, **Ryokan Hiramatsu** would be an excellent choice for those specific nights.

For a mix of modern comfort and shopping convenience, **HOTEL THE FLAG Shinsaibashi** is a solid alternative, especially if the client decides to spend a few nights in Osaka.
